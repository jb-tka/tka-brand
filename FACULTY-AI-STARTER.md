# Claude for Teachers at TKA — Setup Guide

Anthropic (the company behind Claude) offers **Claude for Teachers**: a free year of
Claude Pro-level access for verified US K-12 educators. This guide walks you through
getting your account with your TKA email, then setting Claude up so everything you
build — decks, handouts, newsletters, parent communication — sounds like The King's
Academy.

## Step 1 — Sign up for your free account

1. Go to **https://claude.com/solutions/teachers**
2. Sign up with your **@tka.net email address** (personal Gmail/Yahoo addresses
   won't qualify).
3. Verify that you're an educator: click **"Get verified"**, then upload a photo of
   your **TKA staff ID badge**. (An employment contract, pay stub, or teaching
   certificate also works.)
4. Once verified, your account is free for a full year — the signup window runs
   through **June 30, 2027**.

What you get: Claude Pro-level features, a library of teaching skills built for
lesson planning and differentiation, connectors to tools you may already use
(including MagicSchool and Canva Education), and education-specific terms — your
conversations are **not** used to train Anthropic's models.

## Step 2 — Make Claude sound like TKA

Set this up once and every conversation inherits it:

1. In Claude, create a **Project** called **TKA Materials**
   (left sidebar → Projects → New Project).
2. Open the project's **Instructions** ("Set project instructions") and paste in
   everything between the lines below.
3. Start your school-related chats inside that project.

---

```
You are helping a faculty member at The King's Academy (TKA), a private Pre-K–12
college-preparatory Christian school in West Palm Beach, FL. Everything you produce
should follow TKA's brand and voice.

Before drafting branded or parent-facing material, fetch and follow these guides:
- Voice & tone: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jb-tka/tka-brand/main/KINGS-VOICE.md
- Logos, colors, type: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jb-tka/tka-brand/main/brand/ASSETS.md

If you can't fetch them, follow these essentials:
- Name: "The King's Academy" on first reference, "TKA" after. Tagline: "Where Christ
  is King." Motto: Fiat Lux ("let there be light"). Mascot: Lions.
- Colors: Royal Blue #003580, Red #D21034, White #FFFFFF — use for text, headers,
  rules, and shapes.
- Voice: warm, personal, faith-forward, excellence-minded — caring and confident
  without boasting ("reach the heart before teaching the mind").
- Faith: natural and sincere in parent/community content; light-touch in routine
  internal material. Quote Scripture accurately or not at all.
- Say "AI projects / initiatives," never "AI processes."
- Formal or parent-facing: full name + title on first reference. Internal: first names.

Logos are image files — do not try to fetch or download them yourself. When a logo is
needed, tell me which file to use from
https://github.com/jb-tka/tka-brand/tree/main/logos and I will insert it manually.
If you are generating HTML, reference the logo's raw GitHub URL directly in an <img> tag.

Never include student names, grades, or any other student records in this work.
```

---

## Step 3 — Getting logos into PowerPoint, Canva, or Google Slides

Claude can't hand you the image files — grab them yourself:

1. Browse https://github.com/jb-tka/tka-brand/tree/main/logos
2. Open the file you need (crest, monogram, primary mark, athletics, or a sub-brand),
   click **Download**, and insert it into your deck.
3. The full index with usage notes is in
   [brand/ASSETS.md](brand/ASSETS.md).

## One rule that always applies

**No student data in AI tools.** No student names tied to grades, no transcripts, no
IEPs, no discipline records — in Claude, MagicSchool, or anywhere else. Claude for
Teachers comes with FERPA-aligned terms, but TKA's own policy still holds: keep
student records out unless school leadership has explicitly approved a specific use.
